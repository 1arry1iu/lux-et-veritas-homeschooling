## How to Use This Guide <br /> 如何使用本指南

A useful shortlist normally starts with the **exit route**, then works backward.

一个实用的择校流程通常应该从**最终升学/毕业路径**反推，而不是先看学校名气。

1. **Choose the likely qualification route.** IB, Cambridge/IGCSE/A Level, American/AP, Thai curriculum, Singapore pathway, or another national system.  
   **先确定毕业与升学体系。** IB、剑桥/IGCSE/A Level、美式/AP、泰制、新加坡制或其他国家体系。
2. **Check continuity.** A school may use one framework in primary and a different qualification in secondary.  
   **检查课程连续性。** 小学采用的课程和高中最终资格可能完全不同。
3. **Check language of instruction and language support.** “International” does not automatically mean the same English-language environment at every stage.  
   **确认授课语言及语言支持。** “国际学校”并不代表每个阶段的英语环境都相同。
4. **Evaluate academic fit, not just difficulty.** Consider proof/problem solving, STEM, humanities, arts, languages, learning support, and student independence.  
   **看学术匹配，而不只是“难不难”。** 包括 STEM、人文、艺术、语言、学习支持和自主学习要求。
5. **Look at university preparation.** Examine actual subject availability, counseling, standardized testing, and recent destinations—not just logos on a poster.  
   **评估大学准备。** 看真实选课、升学指导、标化考试与近年去向，而不是只看宣传海报上的大学 Logo。
6. **Visit in person.** Commute, air quality management, class size, teacher stability, campus culture, and safeguarding can matter as much as curriculum labels.  
   **尽量实地访校。** 通勤、空气质量管理、班级规模、师资稳定性、校园文化和学生保护都可能与课程同样重要。

## Curriculum & Qualification Legend <br /> 课程与资格速查

| Term | Meaning | 中文说明 |
|---|---|---|
| **Thai Curriculum** | Thai national/basic education curriculum | 泰国国家基础教育课程 |
| **EYFS** | Early Years Foundation Stage | 英格兰幼儿阶段课程框架 |
| **IPC / IEYC** | International Primary / Early Years Curriculum | 国际小学 / 幼儿课程框架 |
| **Cambridge Primary / Lower Secondary** | Cambridge curriculum frameworks before external secondary qualifications | 剑桥小学 / 初中课程框架 |
| **IGCSE** | International General Certificate of Secondary Education | 国际普通中等教育证书，常见于 Year 10–11 |
| **AS / A Level** | UK-style upper-secondary qualifications | 英制高中阶段资格，常用于大学申请 |
| **IB PYP / MYP / DP / CP** | IB Primary Years / Middle Years / Diploma / Career-related Programmes | IB 小学 / 中学 / 文凭 / 职业相关项目 |
| **Common Core (CCSS)** | U.S. standards commonly used for English language arts and mathematics | 美国共同核心标准 |
| **NGSS** | Next Generation Science Standards | 美国新一代科学标准 |
| **AERO** | Internationally used U.S.-oriented standards framework | 美式国际学校常用标准框架 |
| **AP** | College Board Advanced Placement courses/exams | 美国大学先修课程与考试 |
| **Singapore Curriculum** | Singapore-derived primary/secondary curriculum | 新加坡课程体系 |

> [!NOTE]
> A **curriculum framework** is not automatically a **leaving qualification**. For university planning, distinguish what students *study* from what formal qualification or transcript they *graduate with*.

# Key Pathways <br /> 主要升学路径

## Full / Broad IB Pathways <br /> IB 路径

- **[Prem Tinsulanonda International School](https://ptis.ac.th/)** — IB **PYP + MYP + DP + CP**. The IB directory currently lists Prem as authorized across all four programmes. [IB profile](https://www.ibo.org/en/school/001366/)
- **[American Pacific International School](https://www.apis.ac.th/)** — IB **PYP + MYP + DP**. [IB profile](https://www.ibo.org/en/school/002907)
- **[Panyaden International School](https://www.panyaden.ac.th/)** — British-standards/inquiry-based pathway in earlier years leading to the **IB Diploma Programme** in upper secondary; the school also integrates Thai curriculum elements and its Buddhist values framework.

### Best suited to families prioritising <br /> 更适合重视

International portability, inquiry, extended writing/research, broad subject development, and an IB-style university preparation pathway.

国际可迁移性、探究式学习、研究与长篇写作、跨学科能力，以及 IB 式大学申请路径。

## British / Cambridge Pathways <br /> 英制 / 剑桥路径

- **[Lanna International School](https://www.lannaist.ac.th/)** — National Curriculum of England + IEYC/IPC + Cambridge pathway; British-oriented education from early years through secondary.
- **[Singapore International School, Chiangmai Campus](https://sisb.ac.th/singapore-international-school-chiangmai/)** — EYFS → Singapore Primary → Singapore/Cambridge secondary → **Cambridge IGCSE → AS/A Levels** at the Chiang Mai campus.
- **[Varee Chiangmai International School](https://vcis.ac.th/vcis/)** — English National Curriculum aligned; Cambridge pathway with **IGCSE → AS/A Level**.
- **[Montfort College](https://www.montfort.ac.th/)** — Thai school with international/bilingual options. Current secondary information includes Cambridge-framed Mathematics, Science and English in its bilingual track, with IGCSE/A Level opportunities in its English-program route.

### Best suited to families prioritising <br /> 更适合重视

Structured subject progression, external examinations, early subject specialization, and a familiar route toward UK/Commonwealth universities.

清晰的学科递进、外部统一考试、高中阶段专业化选课，以及英国/英联邦大学路径。

- **[Unity Concord International School (UCIS)](https://www.ucis.ac.th/)** — American curriculum based on California standards, **Common Core + NGSS + AERO**, with **AP** courses in high school.
- **[Americana Chinese International School](https://www.acis.ac.th/)** — listed in the original directory as Common Core/AP-oriented; verify the current programme directly with the school.
- **[Grace International School](https://gisthailand.org/)** — listed in the original directory as an American-oriented/AP pathway; verify current course offerings directly with the school.

### Best suited to families prioritising <br /> 更适合重视

Flexible course selection, GPA/transcript-based progression, AP acceleration, and U.S.-style college preparation.

灵活选课、GPA/成绩单体系、AP 加速课程，以及美式大学申请准备。

# What to Compare Beyond Curriculum <br /> 除课程体系外还应比较什么

A curriculum label is only one variable. For a serious shortlist, compare each candidate on the same dimensions:

| Dimension | Questions to Ask |
|---|---|
| **Academic pathway** | What exact qualification does the student leave with? Which subjects are actually available? |
| **University preparation** | Is there dedicated counseling? SAT/ACT/IELTS support? AP/IB/A Level subject breadth? Recent destinations? |
| **Mathematics & science** | Is acceleration possible? Are advanced courses actually run every year? What happens to unusually strong students? |
| **Languages** | English-medium proportion? Thai requirements? Chinese/French/German options? EAL support? |
| **Learning support** | What SEN/EAL/counseling services exist, and are places capped? |
| **Class size** | What is the actual average and maximum by stage? |
| **Teacher stability** | What proportion of teachers return year to year? What qualifications/experience are expected? |
| **Assessment culture** | Coursework-heavy, exam-heavy, inquiry/project-based, or mixed? |
| **School culture** | Competitive, pastoral, religious, secular, multilingual, sustainability-focused, etc.? |
| **Location** | Daily travel time in real Chiang Mai traffic? Transport available? |
| **Air quality** | Indoor filtration? AQI policy? Sports/activity modifications during smoke season? |
| **Cost** | Tuition + registration + capital/development fees + transport + meals + exams + devices + trips? |

# Homeschooling Perspective <br /> 家庭教育视角

For **Lux et Veritas Homeschooling**, local schools do not have to be viewed only as all-or-nothing enrollment choices. They can also function as **reference systems** for:

- age/grade calibration;
- curriculum sequencing;
- examination and qualification planning;
- extracurricular benchmarking;
- social, sports, arts, and community opportunities;
- university counseling benchmarks;
- identifying when an external course, tutor, exam centre, or school-based programme adds value beyond home study.

对于家庭教育，本指南的价值不仅是“选哪所学校”，还可以用于建立**外部基准**：比较同龄学习进度、规划考试资格、寻找社交/体育/艺术资源，以及判断什么时候值得引入外部课程、导师、考试中心或学校项目。

## Maintenance Notes <br /> 维护说明

When updating this file:

1. Prefer **official school, examination-board, accreditation, or government sources**.
2. Record the **curriculum framework** separately from the **terminal qualification**.
3. Do not infer current AP/IB/A Level subjects from old school profiles; offerings can change annually.
4. Treat university-offer pages as evidence of outcomes, **not a complete measure of school quality**.
5. Avoid ordinal rankings unless the ranking methodology, variables, weights, and date are explicitly documented.
6. Preserve historical links only when they add useful longitudinal evidence; otherwise prefer the newest official profile.
